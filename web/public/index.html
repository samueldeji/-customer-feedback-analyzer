See what customers complain about, every week.

Feedback from our website form, email and Google reviews is tagged by AI. Repeated complaints are grouped, angry customers trigger an instant alert, and the top 5 issues are emailed every Monday.

How it works

  1. 1. A comment arrives from the website form, email or a Google review.
  2. 2. AI tags it as praise, complaint, bug or idea, scores the sentiment and groups it with similar complaints.
  3. 3. If the customer is angry, staff get a Telegram alert straight away.
  4. 4. Every Monday morning, the top 5 issues are emailed to the manager.
